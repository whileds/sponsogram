# Sponsogram Creator Incubation Program

## SERVICES
* **Content Ideation Strategy**: we will help creators to make content plans based on what is trending and new things in their niche scripting.
* **Video Editing Support**: In 1 week max 2 videos will be edited by our team for them.
* **Personal Manager**: He will manage his social media account across all platforms and will be POC for incubation plans by Sponsogram. Will reply on creators behalf. And if any external conflict occurs, the personal manager will resolve it. Tell creator improvisations if required. (If content is ok, he will forward to editing team)
* **Access to Sponsogram Toolkit all tools for free**
* **Brand Collaboration Support**
* **Free Consultation from exclusive creators (Mentoring)** from their niche.
* **Personal Mentor**: In every 15 days strategy session to boost engagement and virality (from Sponsogram Team). Advise on content, target setting. Reevaluation of every reel. Telling Ways to earn as a creator.
* **Access to Creator Community niche wise and main**: Putting spotlight on the best performance video.
* **Weekly Matchmaking with relatively big creators**: Big and small creator collaboration and matchmaking every week for 1 content piece (relatively big)
