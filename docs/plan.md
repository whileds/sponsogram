# Sponsogram Creator Incubation Program - Master Plan

This document outlines the complete module phase-wise plan for building the **Sponsogram Creator Incubation Platform**.

---

## 📌 Phase 1: Landing Page & Marketing Website (Foundation)
**Goal:** Create a high-converting, visually stunning landing page to showcase the incubation program, benefits, pricing packages, and future services.

- **Module 1.1: Design System & Shared Components**
  - Modern color palette, dark mode aesthetics, glassmorphism UI, typography (Inter/Outfit), responsive navbar, and footer.
- **Module 1.2: Hero & Value Proposition**
  - Engaging headline, high-impact CTA ("Apply for Incubation"), dynamic visuals, and key metrics/highlights.
- **Module 1.3: Services Showcase**
  - Detailed features overview: Content Ideation Strategy, Video Editing Support, Personal Manager, Toolkit Access, Brand Collaboration, Free Mentorship, etc.
- **Module 1.4: Pricing & Packages Comparison**
  - Interactive pricing table comparing **Creator Lite**, **Creator Plus**, and **Creator Premium**.
  - Feature matrix highlighting toolkit credit limits, video editing quotas, and brand deal limits.
- **Module 1.5: Creator Community & Spotlight**
  - Section showcasing top-performing creator videos, community spotlight, and matchmaking benefits.
- **Module 1.6: Future Services Teaser & FAQ**
  - Teaser for Content Creation Kit, PR Team, Content Creation Cafe, and an interactive FAQ accordions.

---

## 📌 Phase 2: Creator Application & Onboarding
**Goal:** Streamline the creator onboarding flow and authentication.

- **Module 2.1: Creator Application Flow**
  - Multi-step form for creators to submit channel links, follower counts, niche, content goals, and desired package.
- **Module 2.2: User Authentication & Auth Portal**
  - Secure authentication setup (Email/Password, Social Auth).
- **Module 2.3: Application Status Portal**
  - User status screen (Under Review, Approved, Waitlisted).

---

## 📌 Phase 3: Creator Dashboard & Core Services
**Goal:** Dedicated portal for onboarded creators to access their active benefits.

- **Module 3.1: Dashboard Overview**
  - Key metrics, assigned Personal Manager & Mentor details, next strategy session timer.
- **Module 3.2: Toolkit Access & Credit System**
  - Access to Sponsogram Toolkit with real-time credit tracking (e.g., 100 credits for Lite vs unlimited for Plus/Premium).
- **Module 3.3: Video Editing Request & Status Tracker**
  - Interface for creators to upload raw footage and track editing pipeline (1-2 videos/week depending on plan).
- **Module 3.4: Content Ideation & Strategy Hub**
  - Repository of trending topics, niche strategy plans, and reel re-evaluations from the mentor team.

---

## 📌 Phase 4: Brand Collaborations & Community Hub
**Goal:** Manage brand deals, creator matchmaking, and mentorship bookings.

- **Module 4.1: Brand Collaboration Board**
  - Opportunities listing with monthly application limits enforced (e.g., 4 brands/month for Creator Lite).
- **Module 4.2: Creator Matchmaking Portal**
  - Bi-weekly/Weekly collaboration matchmaking algorithm & request system between creators.
- **Module 4.3: Mentorship & Consultation Scheduler**
  - Automated booking calendar for 15-day strategy sessions and creator mentor consultations.

---

## 📌 Phase 5: Future Integrations & Admin Portal
**Goal:** Scalable admin operations and future expansion modules.

- **Module 5.1: Admin Operations Management**
  - Manager dashboard to review video editing queues, assign personal managers, and approve brand deals.
- **Module 5.2: Content Creation Kit & PR Booking**
  - Portal for booking equipment kits and PR services.
